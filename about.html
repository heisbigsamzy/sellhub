About SellHub

Made for Nigerian businesses

SellHub is an online marketplace where Nigerian businesses open a store, list products, and get paid, without needing to code or hire a designer.

Why we built it

Too many good businesses lose sales to DM screenshots and "is this seller legit?" doubts. SellHub gives every seller a proper store link, and gives every customer a way to see who they are buying from.

How we build trust

  • Sellers are checked. A seller has to submit a government ID and a selfie, and our team reviews them before they can post products.
  • Badges mean something. Identity, bank and business badges only appear after our team has reviewed the documents.
  • Payments go through Paystack. We never store your card details.
  • Problems get reviewed. Customers can report an order, and our team looks into it. See Customer Protection.

Who is behind SellHub

SellHub was founded by Samuel Joshua Maiwuya and is run from Nigeria.
