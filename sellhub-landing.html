Questions

Quick answers.

Is it really free to open a store?

Yes. Opening a store costs ₦0 and needs no card. A small service fee applies to orders and withdrawals, and it is always shown before you confirm.

How do I get paid?

When a customer orders, your share is added to your SellHub wallet. You can withdraw it to your bank account.

How do I get verified?

Confirm your phone number with a code, then submit your ID and bank details. Our team reviews every seller before the badges are added.

Do customers need an account?

Yes, a free customer account. It lets them pay from their wallet and track every order.

What if something goes wrong with an order?

Customers can report a problem from their account and our team steps in. See the Help Center for how this works.
